## Music

**`public/sound/night.mp3`** — the piece on the DR-1's tape (PLAN-3.md 2.2).

- **Work:** Frédéric Chopin, from the *Études*, Op. 10 (composed 1829–1832; public domain).
- **Recording:** from Musopen — <https://musopen.org/music/610-etudes-op-10/>.
- **Licence:** public domain, as marked on the recording's Musopen page (the Creative Commons public
  domain mark; checked by the project owner on 2026-09-26). Musopen releases its Chopin recordings
  (the *Set Chopin Free* project) into the public domain; the same collection is listed as CC0 on the
  Internet Archive: <https://archive.org/details/musopen-chopin>.
- **Used as:** the original MP3 (172.5 s, 192 kbps), unedited; level, compression and the loop are
  applied at playback (`src/night/music.js`).
